How it works

  1. 1

    Buyer locks ETH

    The buyer creates an escrow with the seller, a neutral arbiter and a deadline. The ETH moves into the contract, not to the seller.

  2. 2

    Seller delivers

    The seller ships the item or finishes the work and marks the escrow as delivered on-chain.

  3. 3

    Buyer releases

    The buyer confirms and the contract pays the seller instantly. Every step is recorded on the blockchain.

No one can stall the deal

If the seller never delivers, the buyer reclaims the funds after the deadline. If the buyer goes silent after delivery, the seller can claim payment after the review period. Nobody can lock the money forever.

Disputes go to an arbiter

Either side can freeze the escrow. Only the arbiter both parties chose can decide who gets paid — and if they never do, the buyer is refunded.

Rules enforced by code

Role checks, a strict state machine and reentrancy protection mean funds can only move the way the rules allow — exactly once.
